# ADR-001: Select ETAS as the Sole Baseline

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-19

## Decision

Use the spatial-temporal ETAS implementation exercised by EarthquakeNPP as the
only literature target during the baseline phase.

## Consequences

The project must first reproduce a published ETAS experiment and then build an
independent implementation. STEP and custom Earthquake Automata models are out
of scope until ETAS alignment is complete.

ETAS provides a demanding benchmark and a natural likelihood-based comparison
for future residual or emergence models.
